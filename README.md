Electric vehicles (EVs) are rapidly transforming modern transportation, but their large-scale adoption depends heavily on the reliability and accurate monitoring of battery systems. Predicting critical battery health indicators—State of Charge (SOC), State of Health (SOH), and Remaining Useful Life (RUL)—remains challenging due to limited, noisy, and imbalanced real-world battery datasets. Additionally, battery degradation follows complex nonlinear patterns with strong temporal dependencies, which traditional predictive models often fail to capture effectively.
To overcome these limitations, this paper presents a hybrid framework that combines Time-series Generative Adversarial Networks (TimeGAN) for synthetic data augmentation with a Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) for robust battery state prediction. TimeGAN generates realistic multivariate time-series data that preserves temporal behavior and inter-feature relationships, helping to mitigate data scarcity and imbalance. The authenticity of the generated data is validated using t-SNE visualization,statistical similarity measures to ensure close alignment with real battery signals. The augmented dataset is then used to train the TFT model, which leverages attention mechanisms and gated architectures to model long-term dependencies and nonlinear degradation trends while simultaneously predicting State of Charge (SOC), State of Health (SOH), and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) .
The proposed framework enhances predictive accuracy and robustness, contributing to the development of more reliable and interpretable battery management systems that support safer, efficient, and sustainable electric mobility.
